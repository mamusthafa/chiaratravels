About Chiara Travels Coorg

Your trusted taxi partner for all transportation needs in Madikeri and Coorg

Our Story

Founded in Madikeri, Chiara Travels Coorg has been serving locals and tourists alike with reliable, safe, and comfortable taxi services for years. We understand the local terrain, the roads, the people, and the unique charm of Coorg, allowing us to deliver the best travel experiences.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, affordable, and comfortable transportation while showcasing the beauty of Coorg to every visitor. We are dedicated to customer satisfaction and safety above all else.

Verified Drivers

All drivers undergo background checks and have extensive local route expertise.

Well-Maintained Cabs

Clean, sanitized vehicles ranging from hatchbacks to luxury MPVs for your comfort.

Transparent Pricing

Fixed fares for popular routes with no surprise charges or toll-fee discrepancies.

24/7 Availability

Book anytime - we are here when you need us, day or night, for local or outstation travel.

Our Extensive Service Area

We serve tourist hubs, railway transits, homestays, and urban routes throughout the entire Kodagu (Coorg) district, including:

Madikeri • Kushalnagar • Virajpet • Somwarpet • Gonikoppal • Suntikoppa • Napoklu • Hudikeri • Ammathi • Ponnampet
