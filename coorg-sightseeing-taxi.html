Sightseeing FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answer on tour customization, lunch stops and vehicle details.

Can we customize the sightseeing itinerary?

Absolutely! Our drivers are flexible. You can coordinate your preferred sequence of locations and spend as much time as you like at each spot within the package duration.

Are meals and entry tickets included in the package?

No, the taxi sightseeing package only includes vehicle rent, driver charges, fuel, toll booths, and parking tickets. Food, hotel stays, and entry/activity tickets are to be borne by the passengers.

What happens if we extend past the 9-hour limit?

No worries! Extra hours are charged at nominal rates (e.g. ₹200 to ₹300 per hour depending on the vehicle class chosen). You can discuss extensions directly with the driver or our support desk.
