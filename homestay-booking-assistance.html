Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries answered about homestay booking assistance in Coorg.

Do homestay charges include local home-cooked meals?

Most homestay packages include hot, fresh breakfast. Lunch and dinners (traditional Kodava pork curry, bamboo shoot curry, rice rotis) can be prepared by host families on request and are billed extra at very reasonable rates.

Is homestay booking support free?

Yes! We provide homestay booking assistance absolutely free of charge to tourists who book their Coorg sightseeing taxi or airport transfers with Chiara Travels.

Are these homestays registered with the tourism department?

Yes. We only recommend government-approved and police-verified homestays. This ensures standard hygiene, proper safety measures, and transparent service quality during your visit.
