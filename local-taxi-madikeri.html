Booking Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast responses to common queries regarding local taxi bookings.

Can I book a local taxi for a full day of sightseeing?

Yes! For visiting multiple local attractions, we recommend our specialized Coorg Sightseeing Packages rather than basic hourly rates, as they offer much better value for money.

Do local taxi bookings include driver allowances?

Yes, local hourly and point-to-point rates include the driver's service allowance. There are no additional driver allowances unless the booking runs into late night hours (after 10 PM).

Are the drivers familiar with remote homestays in Coorg?

Our local drivers have years of local estate road navigation experience. They are highly familiar with major and remote homestays, resorts, and campsites across Kodagu district.
