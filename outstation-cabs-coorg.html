Transit FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Read about details on night charges, extra kilometers and interstate borders.

What if I need to change destinations or extend my trip?

We can customize your route on the fly. Extra kilometers are billed based on standard per-km rate variables corresponding to the selected class (Sedan/SUV Ertiga/Innova Crysta).

Are all cabs equipped with air conditioning?

Yes, all our outstation vehicles come with fully functional, high-performance air conditioners. Please note that AC may be switched off in ghat sections on passenger request or to ensure engine safety on steep incline sections.

What are the night travel driver allowances?

For journeys extending between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, a nominal driver night allowance of ₹300 per night is applicable. This is added to your total bill invoice.
